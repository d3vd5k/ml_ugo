## Findings reviewed for retention

- **Logistic Regression versus F1:** slightly lower mean AP, but higher minority F1/recall/MCC and lower diagnostic cost.
- **Random Forest versus F1:** lower mean AP/minority F1/recall/MCC and higher diagnostic cost.
- **LightGBM versus F1:** higher mean AP/minority F1/MCC, unchanged mean recall and almost unchanged diagnostic cost.
- **Versus U5:** all three families gain AP/MCC but lose recall and raise diagnostic cost. LR also loses minority F1; RF/LightGBM gain minority F1. These are descriptive shared-holdout tradeoffs, not significance claims or an automatic inclusion decision.
- F1 locks its U2-selected learner/allocation; the composition locks its U5-selected learner/allocation and validates its own tau. The comparison includes those selections and scoring differences, not a forced same-learner single-knob intervention.
- All selected inner/outer original proposals and ancestry match canonical U5, in both first and later rounds (60 alignment rows, 30,000 overlapping proposals). This is verified behavior of these fixed-OOF trajectories, not an assumption imposed on arbitrary iterative experiments. The accepted training sets differ because U5 relocates while F1 rejects originals.
- The smallest original minority group has real support 24 and held-out support 4. None of the 15 outer composition cells removes all its proposals; all predict the four held-out positives correctly. That tiny cohort does not establish population-level tail protection.
- The composition adds 30 physical learner fits per cell (15 OOF + 15 downstream), compared with 15 for F1; canonical source costs are additional shared attribution. F1 may pay the shared flow fit before the composition reuses it, so incremental cell wall times alone are not an equal-work comparison. No new density/support model is fitted for the composition.

## Scope and mechanism

U5_F1 composes canonical U5 (fixed real-only three-fold OOF true-label-margin difficulty and U2 KMeans/stability/capped allocation) with retained F1 frozen real-only FlowGMM compatibility on original candidates. Flow decisions replace neighbor post-detection and relocation. No F2 density cutoff, refill, or F2 rerun is included.
Ten joint U5 learner/allocation proposals are selected using real inner validation, then locked while four F1 tau proposals are evaluated. Original validation and test rows never train the frozen flow. Inner OOF models exclude inner validation/test; outer OOF refits use all real outer-training rows, including the former inner validation, as in unchanged U5 downstream access. Reserved flow-calibration rows do not fit the flow or supply a support cutoff, but remain accessible to downstream/OOF classifiers.
Every tau trajectory physically fits its own three real-only OOF models, fixed within that trajectory. Canonical U5 and combo OOF probabilities/scores match for the same learner/role; this is score equality, not hidden model-fit reuse.
The unchanged pinned F1 flow scores a one-hot-dequantized continuous-density proxy in detector coordinates; numeric counts remain continuous. Compatibility is a model-based check, not a guarantee of label correctness or true population support. F1 detector fitting/private preprocessing excludes real calibration, validation and test rows even during outer replay.

## All metrics: mean ± sample SD

Twenty-two numeric fields include classifier threshold and the ROC FPR limit. AP and stored primary_metric_value are verified aliases of average_precision, not counted twice; AP is retained as a separate CSV alias. Precision is TP/(TP+FP), zero when no positives are predicted; accuracy is (TP+TN)/N. SD uses ddof=1 over five paired seeds on one shared holdout, not independent folds. Diagnostic cost is 10*FP+500*FN, not an established Shoppers business loss; ROC recall@5% FPR is descriptive, not a deployed threshold selected on test. Runtime/memory are not predictive metrics. Higher is favorable except FP, FN, cost and FPR; threshold/counts are descriptive. No significance or retention decision is made.
