# H1 Shoppers evaluation — user-discarded

## Scope and comparability

- Full iterative run: `results/runs/20261007T153639-17e4169cca5e`; **75/75 completed** (five variants × three families × five seeds).
- Saved-pool control run: `results/runs/20261007T151450-ab30c8827f99`; **120/120 completed** (canonical U2, U2 replay, H1 replay and five thinning repeats per family/seed).
- U5 comparison: the 15 historical U5 cells in `results/runs/20261006T060142-8e042c6ffcf0`; U5 was not rerun or reintroduced into H1.
- Shoppers only; LR, RF and LightGBM; seeds 0–4; identical dataset, outer/inner split fingerprints and ten learner proposals. Fresh no-resampling/original UGO/corrected UGO/U2 predictions are checked against the corresponding historical U5-study references.
- H1 first locks the canonical U2 learner/group-alpha selected by the ten joint proposals, then validates the 12 independent HDBSCAN parameter pairs (`min_cluster_size=5,10,20,40`; `min_samples=1,5,10`). This is extra detector tuning and compute, not a larger learner search.
- Detector fitting uses real minority training rows only, with training-fitted standardization and categorical one-hot scaling by `1/sqrt(2)`. Frozen `hdbscan==0.8.44` approximate prediction gates final post-relocation coordinates. Real noise-labeled rows stay in classifier training; H1 does not guarantee label correctness or true support.
- Fixed 1,000 proposals and 20 generation rounds; rejected quotas are not refilled. Report actual accepted counts rather than claim equal augmentation. All-rejected rounds terminate without unchanged final refitting. Iterative proposal pools may diverge after classifier retraining.
- All displayed summaries are mean ± sample SD across five paired sampler/classifier seeds. The outer test observations are shared, so these are descriptive variations, not five independent test datasets or significance claims. Thinning summaries first average the five repeats within each sampler seed.
- Classifier threshold is fixed at 0.5. AP is sklearn average precision. `recall_at_fpr_test`/`roc_attained_fpr_test` describe attainable test ROC points at FPR≤0.05; they do not select a deployable threshold. `cost=10FP+500FN` is a diagnostic carried over from the existing reports, not a Shoppers business cost. Accuracy and minority precision are derived from the saved confusion counts.
- No Git metadata is available; each run has recoverable `source.zip`, source SHA-256 hashes, protocol/study snapshots, selected configurations and split IDs. Stage 1 is not frozen; CLIMB, H2, F1/F2 and new generators remain out of scope.

## Saved-pool mechanism control

U2 replay and H1 replay use the same immutable saved U2 proposal pool and locked learner. H1 checks post-relocation coordinates. Each random-thinning repeat keeps exactly the same accepted count per original source group as H1. No replay regenerates proposals. The saved-pool H1 settings are selected on the immutable inner pool; the iterative study independently selects settings on iterative inner trajectories. They are different declared experiments, not interchangeable trajectories.
Every replay metric, per-repeat result, paired delta and range is in [the pool run](../results/runs/20261007T151450-ab30c8827f99/); `Thinning_mean` averages five repeats per sampler seed before reporting seed SD.

## Findings for your decision

- Relative to U2, H1 increases AP and minority precision in all three families, but reduces minority recall and balanced accuracy in all three. LR also loses F1/MCC; RF F1 is essentially unchanged with higher MCC; LightGBM loses F1 with little MCC change. The fixed-threshold diagnostic cost rises versus U2 in every family because missed positives increase.
- Compared with no augmentation, H1's tree-family AP gains are small and LR AP remains lower. Those comparisons use the same outer test but separately selected no-augmentation learner settings; the saved-pool controls isolate the locked-learner mechanism.
- The mean group-matched random-thinning AP exceeds H1 replay AP in every family. These results do not demonstrate an AP advantage for HDBSCAN selection beyond simply retaining fewer proposals from the same original groups. Other metrics differ, so this is not a claim that thinning dominates every criterion or a significance claim.
- Three iterative cells have no usable outer HDBSCAN clusters and accept no candidates. The smallest original minority group has zero accepted descendants in 14 of 15 cells; real rows remain present, and its reported test recall is based on only four positives per cell. This is a support-protection concern, not evidence of label corruption.

**User decision: discard H1.** After reviewing these comparisons the user explicitly chose not to keep H1 and authorized F1 testing separately. This report and its run artifacts are historical evidence, not a retained Stage 1 configuration. No component combination, Stage 1 freeze or progression is applied.
