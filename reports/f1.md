# F1 Shoppers comparison — user-retained

## Scope and evidence

- Iterative run: `20261007T180310-edddfeea01ac`; 90 fresh cells, plus 15 historical U5 references from `20261006T060142-8e042c6ffcf0`.
- Immutable-pool run: `20261007T172857-728d008031d6`; 60 fresh cells. Pool replay precedes iterative evaluation; their separately selected tau values are not interchangeable.
- LR/RF/LightGBM, sampler/classifier seeds 0–4, identical controlled real outer test (split seed 2026), ten joint canonical U2 learner/group-alpha proposals, fixed classifier threshold 0.5. F1 tau competes over 0.5/0.6/0.7/0.8 on real inner-validation AP after locking U2; all metrics below are outer-test descriptions.
- F1 replaces neighbor post-detection and relocation, scores original proposals, and never refills rejected quotas. N-reject retains the neighbor detector but rejects original proposals rather than relocating. H1 was discarded; F2/H2/U1/U3/U4/U5 are not composed into F1.
- Independently written, source-hashed `flowgmm_tabular_v1`: shared invertible RealNVP, two learned class-associated diagonal Gaussian components, equal priors, real-only both-class balanced likelihood fitting. Six coupling layers, width 64, two hidden layers, 60 epochs, batch 256, Adam .001; no early stopping or retry.
- Detector-only preprocessing fits a stratified 80% of real inner-fit rows; 20% is reserved calibration (seed+7919), unused by F1. Original inner validation and outer test do not fit preprocessing/weights. The same frozen core/roles are retained for outer replay, with an inverse outer-classifier coordinate bridge. All downstream classifiers retain identical real inner-fit/outer-training access.
- Full one-hot uniform dequantization is used for nominal features, not continuous arbitrary category codes. Numeric counts remain continuous as in unchanged U2 interpolation. The model scores a continuous density proxy in detector coordinates; compatibility is not guaranteed label correctness. Content-keyed evaluation noise is shared between classes.
- Acceptance is `log p(x|intended) - log p(x|opposing) >= logit(tau)` with equal priors; the common flow Jacobian cancels analytically. Both logged likelihoods include that Jacobian. This F1 study applies no F2 likelihood/support quantile or calibration filter; the separate F2 implementation and Shoppers comparisons are documented in [f2.md](f2.md).
- All 60 fresh no-augmentation/original-UGO/corrected-UGO/U2 prediction files and selected learner proposals reproduce the historical U5-study references exactly.
- Tables are mean ± sample SD across five paired seeds. The same outer test is reused; these are descriptive comparisons, not independent-fold significance tests. Higher is better for predictive metrics/recall; lower is better for FP/FN/FPR/cost. Diagnostic cost is `10*FP+500*FN`, not a Shoppers business loss.
- `primary_metric_value` equals AP exactly and is retained in raw records; accuracy and minority precision are additionally derived from confusion counts. Test ROC recall@5% FPR is descriptive, not a deployable threshold selected on test.

## Findings underlying the user retention decision

- **Logistic Regression:** F1 is worse than original and corrected UGO on AP, minority F1, recall and MCC. Versus U2 it recovers some AP but sacrifices recall/F1 and increases diagnostic cost.
- **Random Forest:** versus original UGO, F1 trades lower AP for higher minority F1/recall/MCC and lower diagnostic cost; versus corrected UGO the same operating-point metrics improve while AP is slightly lower. Against U2, AP/MCC improve, minority F1 is nearly unchanged and recall falls.
- **LightGBM:** F1 improves mean AP, minority F1/recall and MCC over both UGO references. Against U2, AP/MCC improve but recall falls and minority F1 is essentially unchanged.
- All three F1 families have lower AP than no augmentation, but higher minority F1/recall. Relative to U2, every F1 family has higher diagnostic cost at the fixed classifier threshold. These mixed tradeoffs do not justify an automatic global retention decision.
- The smallest original minority group is never completely filtered in these 15 F1 cells; its test support is only four positives per cell, so perfect observed group recall is limited evidence, not a support or label-correctness guarantee.
- F1 retains substantially fewer proposals than N-reject. The saved pool isolates the handler on the same inputs, but it does not establish a density-quality benefit independent of augmentation dose: N-reject is not count-matched random thinning.
