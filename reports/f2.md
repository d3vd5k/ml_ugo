- F1 selects tau first. F2 then selects q from explicit off/0.01/0.05/0.10 using real inner-validation AP only; exact ties prefer off then lower q. Fixed q diagnostics are not selected on outer performance. Off has no likelihood cutoff; q=0 applies the minimum calibration cutoff and is not a bypass.
- The intended-class likelihood includes the flow Jacobian. F2 is compatibility AND class-specific likelihood >= the real calibration quantile. The same fit/calibration/validation roles are frozen for outer replay; downstream classifiers keep the same real training access. Original proposals never relocate, and rejected quotas never refill.
- Quantile convention: NumPy linear interpolation over all class calibration rows, extended to -infinity endpoints. A category absent from the fit-only vocabulary has zero closed-vocabulary model support (log likelihood -infinity). Unknown calibration rows remain in the rank denominator/counts and have explicit IDs/classes/reasons/score kinds; no rows are dropped and no detector is refitted. Numerical/model NaN/nonfinite errors still fail explicitly. This is a modelling convention for the unchanged F1 representation, not a fabricated finite score.
- Numeric counts remain continuous as in U2; categorical one-hot dequantization scores a model-based continuous-density proxy. Likelihood and compatibility do not guarantee correct labels or true population support.
- Saved-pool thinning samples only F1-accepted originals, without replacement, matching selected-F2 counts separately per original U2 group for inner and outer. The five repetition means are taken within each classifier/sampler seed before mean/sample-SD across five seeds. These are descriptive results on one shared outer holdout (seed 2026), not independent folds or significance tests.
- Classifier threshold is fixed 0.5. Diagnostic cost is 10*FP+500*FN, not an established Shoppers business loss. ROC recall@5% FPR is descriptive test ROC evidence, not a threshold chosen for deployment.
- Every stored/derived metric is reported below; raw primary_metric_value is the AP alias and remains in benchmark records.

## Findings underlying the F1-only retention decision

- Versus F1, selected iterative F2 increases mean AP/minority F1/MCC slightly in all three families, but reduces minority recall and raises diagnostic cost in all three. The paired all-field tables below quantify the tradeoffs; these small descriptive changes are not a significance claim.
- Versus original UGO, F2 is worse on AP/minority F1/recall/MCC/cost for Logistic Regression. Random Forest loses a little AP but improves minority F1/recall/MCC/cost; LightGBM improves all five. Corrected UGO and no-augmentation/U2/U5 references are also included below.
- The immutable-pool control does not establish a consistent advantage over simply reducing the F1 augmentation dose. Per-group matched random thinning has higher mean AP than F2 for LR and LightGBM, and essentially equal AP for RF. F2 versus thinning changes other metrics in mixed directions; all five thinning repetitions and their within-seed means are preserved.
- Real validation selects support-off in 7/15 iterative cells: LR 1/5, RF 4/5, LightGBM 2/5. F2 accepts fewer proposals on average than F1: 451 versus 519 (LR), 601.8 versus 630.6 (RF), 597 versus 649 (LightGBM). Rejected quotas are not refilled.
- Every minority calibration score is finite (244 rows per seed). Five out-of-vocabulary calibration-row occurrences across the seeds (four distinct source rows) are all majority-class and remain explicitly scored as negative infinity. They do not alter the minority-class cutoff.
- The smallest original minority group has real support 24 and held-out support 4. None of the 15 selected iterative F2 cells removes all its synthetic proposals; all predict its four held-out positives correctly. These tiny nearest-real assignment cohorts do not establish population-level tail protection.
- First-round originals/parents/groups match F1 in every selected cell. Retraining changes 45 later original proposals across 7/15 cells; therefore the iterative study is not mislabelled an immutable-pool comparison.
- The extra q search requires additional classifier fits despite zero new flow fits. The subsequent explicit user decision is to retain F1 only and discard F2.
